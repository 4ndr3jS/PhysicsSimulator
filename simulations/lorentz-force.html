📚 Key Concepts
  • Magnetic force is perpendicular to both velocity and field
  • Electric force is parallel to the electric field
  • Positive charges curve opposite to negative charges
  • Magnetic force does no work (speed stays constant)
  • Particles spiral in combined fields
